Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Microphone

The tuner uses the microphone only while it is on. Sound is analysed on your device to measure pitch and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Information we collect

We do not run servers for the app, do not require an account and do not collect personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. On iOS you are asked through App Tracking Transparency whether ads may be personalised; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are requested. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change it any time under "Privacy choices" in the Songs tab. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
